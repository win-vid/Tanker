<h1>Tanker</h1>
In Tanker, the world lies in ruins, consumed by a ruthless, misanthropic super-intelligence. As the last human tank commander, you are humanity’s final line of defense — a lone steel titan waging war against endless legions of AI-driven machines. Every kill secures a heartbeat for humanity.

<h2>About</h2>
Tanker is an endless isometric, single-player, bullet hell video-game in which you controll a tank shooting down hordes of enemies. You are able to use power-ups to your advantage to rain even more carnage upon the deadly enemy war-machines.
This project is part of my introduction to Game Engines cuourse. 

<h2>How to play</h2>
<h3>Movement</h3>
- W, Accelerate
- S, Drive Backwards
- A, Turn Left
- D, Turn Right

<h3>Shooting</h3>
- Turn the canon by moving the mouse cursor
- Press or hold the <bold>left mouse button</bold> to shoot
